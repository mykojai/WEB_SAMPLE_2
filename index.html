05 // DYNAMIC GLOSSARY

"I noticed the Avatars used heavy clinical terminology. Here is the exact human translation."

Translational Hedging

Using filler words like 'well, you know' to buy time while you mentally translate from your native language to English.

Semantic Bleaching

Relying on weak, generic words like 'thing' instead of using the precise, powerful vocabulary you actually know.

Anacoluthon

Starting a complex sentence but abruptly abandoning it halfway through because your brain ran out of processing power.

Conversational Buffering

Flooding the beginning of your sentence with empty phrases to delay getting to the actual point.

Consonant Cluster Epenthesis

Adding extra vowels to words because your articulatory muscles are fatigued or rushing.
